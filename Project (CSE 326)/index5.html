Terms & Conditions

Emergency Shelter Allocation System

  1. Applicants must provide correct registration information.
  2. The system automatically allocates available accommodation.
  3. Applicants must reach their allocated shelter within 5 hours of allocation confirmation.
  4. If an applicant does not arrive within 5 hours, the reservation expires and the bed may be transferred to another person in need.
  5. If additional time is required because of an emergency, the applicant should contact THE HEAD OF CONTROL before the reservation expires.
  6. Families will be allocated in the same room whenever sufficient beds are available.
  7. Animal accommodation is managed separately.
  8. Registration information must be accurate.
  9. THE HEAD OF CONTROL manages shelter capacity and emergency allocation.
